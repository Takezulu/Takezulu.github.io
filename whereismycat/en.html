About

A little room, and the cats who visit

Where Is My Cat? is the story of your cat’s little room, and the cats who come to visit it. Put out food and furniture, then come back to see which cats have stopped by. Watching them laze around the room is the healing part — and it comes free.

Cats keep visiting while the app is closed. Open the game for a moment, see who came by, top up the food, and get on with your day. Available in Korean, English and Japanese.

Features

What you can do

  • Collect the hearts that fall in the room and spend them on furniture and food
  • Play with a laser, pet your cats, and watch each one react in its own way
  • Take photos with the visiting cats and build an album
  • Meet new arrivals, fill your guestbook and complete achievements
